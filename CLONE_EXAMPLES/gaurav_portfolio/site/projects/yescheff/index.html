Creating a Recipe

You can start by searching for what you want to cook or by pasting a link to a cooking video. YesCheff turns the video into a recipe with the ingredients, servings, directions, timings, tools and dietary information brought together in one place. To keep each recipe reliable, YesCheff only uses details it can find in the video captions or transcript. If there is not enough information, it stops rather than adding missing ingredients or cooking steps. Servings can also be adjusted to automatically recalculate compatible ingredient quantities. Measurements that cannot be safely understood, such as “to taste,” are kept unchanged.

Cooking & Recipe Interaction

Recipes are organised into ingredients and directions. The cooking directions are separated into clear steps, so you can focus on one part of the recipe at a time. You can move forward, go back or jump to a particular step without losing where you are. Timers sit within the steps where they are needed, keeping each timer connected to what you are cooking.

Hands-Free

For moments when touching a device is not convenient, voice mode lets you control the recipe using spoken commands. You can start, pause, repeat or move between steps while YesCheff reads the cooking instructions aloud. Voice and touch use the same recipe, so you can switch between them without losing your place. When a step includes a timer, YesCheff reads the instruction first and then asks whether the timer should begin.

Saving, Credits & Membership

Recipes can be saved to your account, so you don't have to generate the same recipe again whenever you want to cook it. Saving recipes uses credits, with more credits available from the Plans page. Becoming a member removes that limit with unlimited saves.

Project info

Cooking from a video often means pausing and going back to find an ingredient, check a quantity or remember what comes next. YesCheff makes this easier by turning cooking videos into recipes that are easier to read and follow while cooking. From brand to product, I designed and built the experience, including recipe creation, serving adjustments, guided cooking steps, timers, hands-free voice controls, saved recipes, credits and membership.
